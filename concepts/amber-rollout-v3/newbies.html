A gentler first step.

A public, vanilla meeting for conversation, curiosity and new connections in Brussels.

The Newbies Munch is a smaller, welcoming introduction for people who want to learn more about BDSM, Munches and the community.

It is designed to make the first step feel easier. You can ask questions, learn what to expect and meet others in a calm setting without needing previous experience.

There is no play, no dress code and no pressure to talk more than you want to. Come in ordinary clothing and take things at your own pace.
