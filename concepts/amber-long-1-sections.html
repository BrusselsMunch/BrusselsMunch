Come for a chat.

A Munch is an informal gathering around BDSM and related kinks. It gives people a chance to meet others in a public, vanilla setting, whether they are experienced, curious or simply interested.

Ask about the next Munch

Everyone is welcome to come for a drink, a chat and new connections. You can talk about BDSM, other kinks, everyday life or nothing in particular. It is completely fine to arrive shy, sit quietly for a while, or bring a friend who is not part of the scene.

There is no play during the Munch, no dress code and no pressure to take part in conversations. We meet in ordinary clothes and behave appropriately for a public venue. Brussels is multilingual, so you will usually hear English, French and Dutch, with other languages sometimes joining in.

The Munch is free to attend. You only pay for anything you choose to eat or drink. At least one member of the Brussels Munch team hosts each evening, and hosts can be recognised by their Brussels Munch badge.

We meet in a central Brussels bar on the last Tuesday of the month, with the December meeting on a different date. The exact location is shared after contact so we can confirm that you have read and accepted the participation terms. We normally reply within a day.
